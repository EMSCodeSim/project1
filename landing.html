STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before you trust it with your career.

Does ResponderLog replace official department records?

No. It is your personal working record. Continue using every form, ePCR, exposure report, training system, and taskbook your department requires.

Can I enter patient information?

No. ResponderLog is deliberately designed for non-identifying career evidence. Never enter names, dates of birth, addresses, medical record numbers, or other patient identifiers.

What happens if I change departments?

Your free record is device-local and portable. Export a backup before changing devices or clearing browser data, then import it wherever you continue your career.

Is the readiness score a guarantee?

No. It is a transparent summary of evidence you have logged, not a certification, clinical judgment, or promise of promotion. Your agency’s standards always control.
