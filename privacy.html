PRIVACY

Your record belongs to you.

The free ResponderLog app stores career information in your browser on your device. It does not require an account and does not send your career entries to a ResponderLog database.

What the free app stores

Career entries, certifications, exposures, goals, taskbooks, profile details, and preferences are stored in your browser’s local storage. You can export a backup or clear all information from Data & Settings.

Information you submit

If you submit the founding-access form, the name, email, plan interest, and optional department size you provide are collected so we can respond to your request. We do not sell this information.

Keep patient information out

ResponderLog is not an ePCR or official reporting system. Do not enter patient names, dates of birth, addresses, medical record numbers, phone numbers, or other identifying information. The app includes pattern checks, but those checks cannot identify every possible privacy risk.

Important limits

Device-local storage can be lost if you clear browser data, replace a device, or remove the app. Download regular backups. Continue to follow all agency requirements for training, exposure, incident, medical, and workers’ compensation documentation.
